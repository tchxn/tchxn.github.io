## The work

A body of printmaking and photography about being from two places at once. Symbolism, strength and duality — not as an abstract theme, but as the ordinary experience of holding a Papua New Guinean identity and an Australian one and finding they don't cancel out.

## What it involved

Beyond the works themselves: an artist profile, a written catalogue describing each piece, the invitation design, a loan agreement for the hanging, and a full exhibition report afterwards.

Curating your own show teaches you something a brief never does — that the work only lands if the room, the sequence and the words around it are also designed.

## Why it's in a systems portfolio

Because it's the same question underneath. Everything else here is about two things that are supposed to be separate and aren't: structure and image, the technical and the human, the office and the warehouse. This is where I said that out loud first.
