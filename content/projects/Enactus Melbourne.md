## The work

Designed user flows, scoped requirements and iterated prototypes for a social-enterprise MVP, working inside a cross-functional student team through to a testable build.

## What I took from it

Working with people who weren't designers, on a deadline nobody owned, taught me to write requirements that survive being handed to someone else. Short, but the first time I had to make a spec do the talking for me.
