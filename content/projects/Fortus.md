## The situation

Fortus had been built screen by screen. Each view solved its own problem in its own way, so hierarchy and interaction patterns drifted apart, and the analytics were spread across views that didn't agree with each other.

## What I did

- Rebuilt **five core interfaces**, resolving the hierarchy and consistency problems rather than restyling them
- Restructured the analytics dashboard across **five data views** so the numbers read as one system
- Built **10+ reusable Figma components** to replace one-off screens, so new views inherit the patterns instead of reinventing them

## Outcome

The client reported a **32% lift in weekly active users** in the month after relaunch, and **1,000+ users within three months**.

That figure is theirs, not mine — I didn't have access to the analytics that produced it, so I quote it as reported rather than claiming it as a measured result of the redesign alone.
