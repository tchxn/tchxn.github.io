## The work

Baseline brand guidelines for Melbourne Vicentre Swimming Club, plus development of an alternate mark alongside the primary logo, a defined palette resolving a red-and-navy contrast problem, and a digital asset kit — logo files, colour codes, typefaces, social templates.

Guidelines are with the board for endorsement, with the alternate mark going to swimmers for feedback.

## The commercial side

This is the project where I learned the business half of design.

- Scoped and quoted the work as a retainer rather than hourly, because the hours aren't the product
- The board **countered** with a different structure. I accepted it, with a turnaround caveat that protected me against the risk their version introduced
- **Retained master and working files**; final deliverables are supplied as PDF, PNG and JPEG, with a buyout priced separately
- Terms agreed in writing before any work began

Design is the easy part. Being trusted with a budget and a board is a different skill, and it's the one that turns a freelancer into a supplier.
