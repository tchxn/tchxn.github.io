## The situation

Students throw away clothes that need small repairs, mostly because mending is inaccessible rather than because they don't care. The brief was sustainability; the actual barrier was access.

## What I did

- Mapped the end-to-end journey, then tested it through **physical walkthroughs** rather than only on screen
- Designed against an explicit **accessibility statement**, so accessibility was a stated constraint rather than a review at the end
- Built a working **Voiceflow chatbot integrated with Airtable**, so a booking made in conversation lands in a real database with fallback and confirmation handling

## Why it belongs here

This gets filed as a design project, but the interesting part is the integration layer — a conversational front end writing into a structured backend, with the failure cases designed rather than ignored. It's service design and systems work in the same object.
