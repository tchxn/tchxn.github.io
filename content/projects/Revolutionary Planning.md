## The situation

RPM Hire hires out traffic equipment for road works. In two discovery sessions with them, I led our team's questions and we found the gap: site speed data was recorded on every job but never reached the plan for the next one.

## What we built

A planning tool scoped around that gap, so each finished job informs the next. I mapped each contractor requirement the team defined to where the prototype met it, and kept that map current through six rounds of team review.

## How we worked

We did our early ideation as a team without AI tools and scaffolded the problem by hand. Everyone built the reasoning in their own head, so everyone could follow it, and we only brought tools in for prototyping.

## Where it stopped

We weren't shortlisted. Sometimes a stronger entry wins. What I took from it is the value of a team that thinks the problem through together before reaching for tools: the reasoning was clearer because we built it ourselves.
