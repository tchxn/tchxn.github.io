Act 01 — Method

I take things apart.

Give me something that isn't working and I'll find where it actually breaks — not where it looks like it breaks. Here's a real one: a handwritten delivery slip from a distribution business in Port Moresby.

01 — As found

order #—
4 × tofu, 2 × noodle
K840 cash?
driver — the usual guy
“left this morning”
paid? ask later

02 — Taken apart
Customeridentified

Deliveredunverified
Cash returnedunreconciled
03 — Put back
✓Packed · warehouse08:12
✓Assigned · dispatcher08:40
✓Delivered · proof captured11:26
✓Cash reconciled · office16:03

The business thought it had a paperwork problem. It had an accountability problem — nothing tied an order to the person, vehicle and outcome behind it. Once that's the question, the answer stops being "an app" and becomes an audit trail everything else has to serve.

Twenty pages, one shape.
